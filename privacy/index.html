License Saver for Jira

Privacy Policy & Data Handling

Last updated: 27 October 2026

License Saver (“the app”) is an Atlassian Forge app published by Marouani Apps (Mohamed Habib Marouani) (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what data the app processes, where it is stored and how to delete it.

In short
  • The app runs entirely on Atlassian’s Forge platform. We operate no servers, and your data is never sent to us.
  • Data is stored only in Atlassian’s Forge storage for your installation.
  • The app communicates only with Atlassian’s own APIs (your Jira site and api.atlassian.com).
  • No third-party analytics, tracking or advertising.

The app does not read issue contents, comments, attachments, Confluence pages or any other work data.

2. Where data is stored

All data is stored in Atlassian Forge storage for your installation, hosted by Atlassian and covered by Atlassian’s security and data-residency commitments for Forge. The organization API key is stored as an encrypted Forge secret; it is never shown again in the app and never sent to the browser. We, the publisher, have no access to your stored data.

3. Data sent outside your site

The app makes outbound requests only to api.atlassian.com (the Atlassian Administration API), using the API key you provided. No data is sent anywhere else.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Scan results are replaced by the next scan.
  • The activity log keeps the most recent 500 actions.
  • You can revoke the stored API key at any time in Atlassian Administration → Settings → API keys.
  • When you uninstall the app, Atlassian deletes its Forge storage according to its Forge data-retention rules.

5. Actions the app can take

The app suspends or restores user access only when an administrator clicks Suspend or Restore, or when an administrator has turned on automatic mode (limited to 25 users per day and never more than 10% of users, skipping users with open issues, new users and recently restored users). Every action is logged and can be reversed. The app’s page is available only to Jira administrators.

6. Sub-processors

Atlassian Pty Ltd (hosting through the Forge platform). No other sub-processors.

7. Your rights (GDPR)

Your organization is the data controller. Atlassian processes the data on the Forge platform. Because we cannot access your data, requests to access or delete personal data are handled by your Atlassian administrator (for example by running a new scan, removing users or uninstalling the app). For any question, contact us below.
